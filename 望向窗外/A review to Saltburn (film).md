# A review to Saltburn (film)  
  
   
   
 #### In this film, I saw very strong class divide between  noblemen and middle-class in British society.   
 #### Even people have already entered a university which aim to cultivating elites for society, like Oxford,   their identity always be distinguished by class they originated from.   
  
 #### A young man who is a freshman at  Oxford University, decided to change his position in society — becoming member of upper class , but using extremely horrible ways.   
  
 #### At the beginning of the film, Oliver Quick fits all the stereotypes of poor and young nerd.   
  
 ####  He seems to be in love with his classmate, Felix Catton, who comes from a noble family, social start at Oxford University.    
  
 ####  No doubt, Oliver has unflinching ambitions to implement his great plan. He is captivated by  wealth and upper-class life. He wants to be one of them.   
 #### The manners that he used including lying, playing people, murder and cheating.   
 ####  On next, let’s explore what he did:   
## Lying
####    Oliver told his classmates he comes from a poor and problematic family - mother is an alcoholic, even his father is a drug deliver.   
####    The truth is Oliver was born in standard middle-class family, his parents are very kind, warm and normal person.  His mother cooked breakfast for welcoming him and Felix.   
           
## Playing people 
        
####   At the beginning of the film, there is a statement from Oliver Quick. He look at camera and attempts to express his love to Felix — we will back later to explain this.   
####   He destroyed relationship between Felix and Felix’s cousin, Farleigh Start by frame-up.   
####   And his operation causes Felix’s father, Sir Catton peel Farleigh away from the castle.   
  
  ## Murder 
  
#### Oliver Quick killed Felix by poison;  
####  He killed Felix’s sister while she was sleeping in bathtub.   
####  At the almost end of the film, we can see Felix’s mother was killed by him also.   
####  Generally, he killed almost whole family of Felix, one by one.  
  
  
 ## Cheating

## Lasting            
####    From Oliver’s background, we cannot really know why he becomes  a murderer.   
#### But at the end of the film, I suddenly realize that the smile on his face when he first sees Felix, is like the smile on predator’s face when it seeing its prey. He said that he loved Felix, he present himself as a guy who was falling in love with Felix. But deep down, he hates upper-class, and he is jealous of their privileges.   
####  On the other hand, Felix’s family does not  care lower-class. They treat lower-class people like animals, with no respect. They treat Oliver like a pet.  
####  I’m not going to judge these people, but I do see a significant social issue in British.
